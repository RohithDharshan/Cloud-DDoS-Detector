Reference paper (Abiramasundari & Ramaswamy, Scientific Reports 2025) reports 98.9% accuracy for Random Forest on real CICIDS2017 (5-day, multi-attack dataset). Figures above are trained on the real CIC-IDS2017 Friday-afternoon DDoS capture (see Dataset section above) — a single-day binary BENIGN-vs-DDoS task, which is why scores land a bit above the paper's multi-day/multi-attack benchmark.
